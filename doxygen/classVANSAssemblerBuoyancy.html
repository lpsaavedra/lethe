Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename PropertiesIndex>
class VANSAssemblerBuoyancy< dim, PropertiesIndex >

Assembler for the buoyancy force on particles in the VANS equations.

The buoyancy force applied to each particle is computed as:

\[\mathbf{F}_b = -\rho_f \mathbf{g} \frac{\pi}{6} d_p^3 \]

which is equivalent to \( -\rho_f \mathbf{g} V_p \), where \( V_p = \frac{4}{3}\pi\left(\frac{d_p}{2}\right)^3 \) is the particle volume, \( \rho_f \) is the fluid density, \( \mathbf{g} \) is the gravitational acceleration, and \( d_p \) is the particle diameter.

Note
The buoyancy force is stored as a one-way coupling force on the particle and does not generate a reaction force on the fluid.
