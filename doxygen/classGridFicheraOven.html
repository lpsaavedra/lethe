Detailed Description

template<int dim, int spacedim>
class GridFicheraOven< dim, spacedim >

Generates a 3D Fichera oven mesh by removing cells from a subdivided hyper-rectangle.

The geometry is a staircase-shaped domain obtained from a 2x2x3 subdivided hyper-rectangle by removing three groups of cells at increasing z-levels.

When colorize is true, boundary faces at the top of the remaining staircase (z = top_right[2]) are assigned boundary_id = 1 (e.g., inlet); all other boundary faces retain the default boundary_id = 0.

template<int dim, int spacedim>
void GridFicheraOven< dim, spacedim >::make_grid ( Triangulation< dim, spacedim > & triangulation)

Generate the Fichera oven mesh by removing cells from a subdivided hyper-rectangle.

Starting from a 2x2x3 subdivided hyper-rectangle spanning from bottom_left to top_right, three groups of cells are removed to create a staircase geometry:

*   Level 1 (z > 1/3 height): remove (+x, +y) quadrant
*   Level 2 (z > 2/3 height): additionally remove (+x, -y) and (-x, +y)
*

The midpoints along x and y are at (bottom_left + top_right) / 2, and the z-thresholds are at bottom_left[2] + height/3 and bottom_left[2] + 2*height/3.

When colorize is true, boundary faces at z = top_right[2] are assigned boundary_id = 1 (e.g., inlet/outlet); all other faces keep the default boundary_id = 0.

Parameters
[out]triangulationThe triangulation to fill with the oven mesh.
