Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename PropertiesIndex>
class VANSAssemblerPressureForce< dim, PropertiesIndex >

Assembler for the pressure force on particles.

The undisturbed pressure force applied to each particle is computed as:

\[\mathbf{F}_p = -\rho_f V_p \nabla p \]

where \( \rho_f \) is the fluid density, \( V_p = \frac{\pi}{6} d_p^3 \) is the particle volume, \( d_p \) is the particle diameter, and \( \nabla p \) is the fluid pressure gradient evaluated at the particle location.

Note
When using VANS Model A, the pressure force is applied only to the particle (one-way coupling). When using Model B, the pressure force is also applied back on the fluid as a reaction force (two-way coupling).
