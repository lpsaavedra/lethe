Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerCore< dim >

Class that assembles the cell terms of the ultraweak DPG formulation of the time-harmonic Maxwell equations. With the test functions \(\mathbf{F}\) (electric) and \(\mathbf{I}\) (magnetic), it assembles:

  • the graph norm of the test space in the Gram matrix \(G\): \((\mathbf{F}, \mathbf{F}) + (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}, \nabla \times \mathbf{F}) + (i\omega\varepsilon \mathbf{F}, i\omega\varepsilon \mathbf{F})\) and the equivalent terms coupling \(\mathbf{F}\) and \(\mathbf{I}\) and for \(\mathbf{I}\) alone;
  • the interior bilinear form in the matrix \(B\): \((i\omega\varepsilon \mathbf{E}, \mathbf{F}) + (\mathbf{H}, \nabla \times \mathbf{F}) + (\mathbf{E}, \nabla \times \mathbf{I}) - (i\omega\mu \mathbf{H}, \mathbf{I})\);
  • the interior load vector \(l\), which is zero since no volume source term is considered at the moment.
